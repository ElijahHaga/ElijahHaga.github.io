Chainsaw Massacre 3lb Robot

This is a 3 pound robot I designed for Combat Robotics Club at MIT, for our compeition at NHRL. It used a chainsaw chain as it's weapon, so I named it Chainsaw massacre after the movie series. Although the bot didn't do very well, it was a great experience designing and assembling it, and I've learned a lot about better design with manufactring in mind because of it. At the end of last semester, I was elected as one of the manufacturing chairs for Combat Robotics Club, and I have been named captain of our new 30 pound robot KRATOS. Although I am studying to be an electrical engineer, I have a lot of experience in machining and mechanical design, which I believe has made me more versatile and a much better engineer as a whole. Because of my experience in Combat Robotics machining on the Haas CNC machines, I was able to help the company I interned at over the summer set up their Haas machine.
