KRATOS (30 Pound Combat Robot)

I am the captain of this 30 pound combat robot, KRATOS, which was designed originally by Anhad Sawhney, a previous team Captain, and which I helped manufacture and took over once he graduated. The robot is a traditional overhead spinner design, but it uses a custom-made axial flux motor to drive its weapon. This allows the robot to overcome the main issue with overhead spinner designs, which is the slow spin-up time of the weapon. Additionally, the ESC used to drive the weapon kept dying due to the massive back EMF caused by the weapon colliding, so I designed a custom ESC which you can learn more about in the VESC project page. The robot has had issue in the past, but is now fully operational and has already qualified for the 2026 NHRL World Championship.
