Custom VESC Project

I am an executive member of the MIT Combat Robotics Team, specifically one of the manufacturing chairs. I am also the captain of our new 30 pound robot, KRATOS, which our previous club president designed last year. One of the main issues I was left with to fix was the poor quality of the commercial motor controllers we were using. The company who mad them based their design on the open-source VESC Project, founded by Benjamin Vedder, so over this summer I have developed an improved version. My verison of the VESC can handle 200 Amps of continuous current at 48V, while giving us more features thn the base VESC design, such as Bluetooth, a built-in opto-isolator, better shielding, and improved protectionary measures. I am still working on this project, and I am currently adding said protectionary measures, including reverse polarity protection, overvoltage and overcurrent protection, and the ability to power the board off of USB-C, which the original VESC didn't have. I will also have to write my own firmware for the bluetooth module as it is an updated chip from the one in the original VESC.
