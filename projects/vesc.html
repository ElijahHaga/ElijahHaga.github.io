Custom VESC Project

I am an executive member of the MIT Combat Robotics Team, specifically one of the manufacturing chairs. I am also the captain of our new 30 pound robot, KRATOS, which our previous club president designed last year. One of the main issues I was left with to fix was the poor quality of our motor controllers from Maytech Innovations. They created their design from the open-source VESC Project, founded by Benjamin Vedder, so over this summer I have developed an improved version. My verison of the VESC can handle 200 Amps of continuous current at 48V, while giving us more features thn the base VESC design, such as Bluetooth, a built-in opto-isolator, better shielding, and better protectionary measures. I am still working on this project, but so far i have finished the design of the boards and I have begun writing the firmware for the Bluetooth chip.
