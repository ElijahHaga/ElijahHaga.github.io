About Me

I am Elijah Haga, a rising junior at MIT majoring in course 6-5 (Electrical Engineering with Computing). I have a lot of experience in applications, such as debugging, reverse engineering, and prototyping. I am mostly focused on RF, but I've done many multi-disciplinary projects, such as a custom 75V, 300A ESC, firmware development for another ESC I designed for an internship, HDL design of a 5-stage pipe-lined RISC-V processor, and design and fabrication of silicon-level chips. This last semester, I designed and fabricated a high-frequency RF power sensor, which uses a zero-bias Schottky diode to rectify RF power above ~65 GHz to a DC output. In addition to my classes and research, I am the Vice Captain for the MIT Combat Robotics Club, which I was previously a manufacturing chair for. I am the captain of the 30 pound robot KRATOS, which uses a custom-made axial flux motor (designed and manufactured by previous team Captain Anhad Sawhney) to drive its 9 pound overhead-style weapon. The bot will also use my custom-made ESC to drive its weapon moving forward. I hope to work more in RF and high-speed logic in the future.
